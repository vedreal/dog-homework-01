Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-the-dog-ate-my-homework-funniest-excuse-8659c98c).

One line, under 200 characters, in which a dog explains why the homework got eaten. Ten fresh candidate excuses are scored against the judge's brief in order — dog's excuse in the dog's own voice, bigger laugh with a twist of dog logic, sounds like a real dog, works on its own, fresh — and the winner is written to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The chosen excuse is written to `/output/entry.txt` (single line, under 200 characters).
